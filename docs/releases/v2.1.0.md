BellForge 2.1 opens straight to the calendar, and creating or deleting bells now runs in the background while you keep working.

## New

- **The calendar comes first.** After logging in, BellForge opens on the current month with today selected. Everything else is in the **Add bells** menu at the top right, and opens in a panel over the calendar.
- **Bells are created and deleted in the background.** Each create or delete is a job, and jobs run one after another while you keep using BellForge: browse other months, or queue more days. The jobs button in the top bar shows progress, lists what's waiting (each can be cancelled before it starts), and what finished.
- **A notice when a job is done.** A notice appears at the top right when a job finishes or stops, with a link to the day. If BellForge isn't the window in front, a Windows or macOS notification appears too.
- **Several days at once.** Shift-click picks every weekday between two days, and Ctrl-click (⌘-click on Mac) adds or removes single days. Then **Apply schedule…** puts one schedule on all of them.
- **Apply a schedule to a day** straight from that day's bells.
- **Your school is remembered.** Its login opens as soon as BellForge starts. **Change**, next to the school's name, picks another.
- **A new launch animation.**

## Changed

- BellForge takes on the school's own color once a school is chosen (maroon for O'Dea).
- The review warns when a day already has bells, since new bells are added alongside them rather than replacing them.
- If the calendar can't be reached partway through, the queue pauses with a **Resume** button instead of failing every job after it.
- Quitting while jobs are left asks first. Jobs only run while BellForge is open.
- A day's bells now sit beside the month instead of under it, and the window opens larger.
- Spacing, text sizes and colors are consistent across every screen, in light and dark.

## Installing

You need to be on the school's network or VPN to reach the calendar.

- **Mac** (Apple Silicon): download `bellforge-v2.1.0-mac.dmg`. The first time, open System Settings > Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway".
- **Windows**: download `bellforge-v2.1.0-windows.exe`. If SmartScreen appears, click "More info", then "Run anyway".
