BellForge 2.2 records who made every change to the bells, and keeps that history on the computer.

## New

- **Who's using BellForge?** When BellForge opens, pick your name or **Create account**: just a name, plus an optional note (like a department) to tell two people with the same name apart. There's no password. BellForge remembers the last person, and your name in the top bar switches person.
- **Activity log.** Every change is recorded: bells created and deleted (including jobs that stopped partway) and schedule types saved and deleted. Each entry shows who made it, when, which school, exactly which bells, how it ended, and which computer it was made on. Open it from your name in the top bar → **Activity log**, and filter by dates and person.
- **Save the log as CSV**, to keep records outside BellForge.

## Changed

- Creating or deleting bells, and saving or deleting schedule types, now needs a person chosen. Looking at the calendar doesn't.
- A notice for a stopped job now closes after 20 seconds instead of staying over the day's bells. The jobs button stays red until you've looked at it, and the job's details stay in the jobs list and the activity log.

The activity log is kept on each computer, for each Windows or Mac user. Nothing in BellForge can edit or remove an entry.

## Installing

You need to be on the school's network or VPN to reach the calendar.

- **Mac** (Apple Silicon): download `bellforge-v2.2.0-mac.dmg`. The first time, open System Settings > Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway".
- **Windows**: download `bellforge-v2.2.0-windows.exe`. If SmartScreen appears, click "More info", then "Run anyway".
